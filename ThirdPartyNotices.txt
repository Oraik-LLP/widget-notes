Liquid-glass shader
-------------------
Assets/GlassyEffect.ps and the WPF ShaderEffect/capture adaptations are based on
WPF-Liquid-Glass-Effect by dragosniamtu contributors:
https://github.com/dragosniamtu/WPF-Liquid-Glass-Effect

The shader and sample code are distributed under the MIT License. The license
text is included in ThirdParty/WPF-Liquid-Glass-Effect-MIT.txt. The capture
helper in this application was adapted to capture only the widget's screen
rectangle. WidgetNotes is an independent Windows implementation and is not
affiliated with Apple or Samsung.

The design reference for edge refraction and chromatic separation is
liquid-glass-react by rdev:
https://github.com/rdev/liquid-glass-react
